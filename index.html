About the game

Idle Defence is a casual idle tower defence game for mobile. Place and upgrade towers, unlock new abilities and watch your defences grow stronger over time.

  • Simple, satisfying tower defence gameplay
  • Idle progress — earn rewards while offline
  • Lots of upgrades to unlock
