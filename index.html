Idle Defence

Defend your base, grow your power across five skill trees and hold back endless waves of enemies — even while you're away.

About the game

Idle Defence is an idle tower defence game for iOS and Android. Your cannon holds the line while you shape it with fire, water, earth and air, collect gear and prestige for permanent power.

  • Five skill trees with active skills, keystones and masteries
  • Items in six slots with element sets and special effects
  • Prestige for shards and a permanent upgrade tree
  • Idle progress — earn rewards while offline
  • Ads only when you choose to watch one
